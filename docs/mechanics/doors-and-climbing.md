# Doors, gates, ladders and stairs

Code:
- `content/world/.../Doors.kt` and `Climbing.kt`.
- Data: `doors.tsv` and `climbs.tsv`, both generated by `cache-dump loc-links` (`tools/cache-dump/.../LocLinks.kt`).
- Translations are adapted from RS Mod's `generic-locs` (ISC; header kept, logged in DECISIONS.md).

Content-specific handlers for the same loc and op win: these are fallbacks (`ContentScope.onOpLocFallback`).

## Doors (DC1–DC4)

- **Pairing.**
  - The cache does not link a door's two states. Their gamevals do: `poordoor` / `poordooropen`,
    `fai_varrock_door` / `fai_varrock_door_open`, `opencastledoubledoorl`, `rustic_openfencegate_l`.
  - The closed loc has op "Open" and the opened loc has "Close". The names match once the state words are removed.
  - A second pass pairs locs that share a display name and models.
  - Result: 387 pairs. 1,155 of the 3,033 wall placements with "Open" stay unpaired; they are quest, minigame and
    locked doors with their own content.
- **DC1. Single doors** (RS Mod `DoorScript`, `DoorTranslations`). Opening turns the door a quarter (angle + 1) and
  moves it one tile into the opening; closing reverses this. A diagonal door that would swing onto the player moves
  them aside first.
- **DC2. Double doors** (`..l` / `..r` names, RS Mod `DoubleDoorScript`). Both halves open outwards together: the left
  half turns +3, the right half +1.
- **DC3. Gates** (`gate` names with `_l` / `_r`, RS Mod `PicketGate`). Both halves swing, and the right half moves two
  tiles.
- **DC4. Other locs.** Non-wall locs with Open/Close (chests, cupboards, trapdoors) swap in place.
- **Revert.** "The (cycle) duration that a door remains changed before reverting to its original state" is 500 ticks
  (RS Mod `DoorConstants.DURATION`).

## Ladders, trapdoors and stairs (DC5–DC7)

- **DC5. Links.**
  - Every placement with a climb op ("Climb-up", "Climb-down", "Climb", and the "Go"/"Walk" up/down variants) is
    linked to the loc on the floor above or below, or 6,400 tiles away for dungeons (RS Mod `DungeonLadderScript`).
    The linked loc must have the opposite op within 5 tiles.
  - The player lands on the walkable tile beside it, nearest the climbed loc, from which the route finder can reach
    the loc.
  - Ladders, stairs and trapdoors with no matching loc use the walkable tile directly above or below.
  - A lone "Climb" (agility rocks, ledges) links only to a matching loc.
  - Result: 4,369 links. 794 placements are unlinked: agility shortcuts and special ropes, which belong to their own
    content.
- **DC6. Climb dialogue.** A lone "Climb" on a loc that also has Climb-up and Climb-down asks. The texts are RS Mod's
  `climbOption`: "Climb up or down the ladder?" / "Climb up or down the stairs?".
- **DC7. Animation.** Ladders play `human_reachforladder` going up and `human_pickupfloor` going down, then move the
  player after a tick (RS Mod `LadderScript`). Stairs move without an animation.
- **Checked:**
  - Lumbridge castle stairs go up to (3205,3209,1) and back down.
  - A door at (3226,3214) opens to (3227,3214) at angle 3, can be walked through, and closes back to its cache state.

## OPEN

- **OPEN-DC1.** Exact landing tiles: OSRS has per-staircase destinations. The nearest reachable tile beside the
  matching loc is the stand-in.
- **OPEN-DC2.** Door sounds: the cache pairs carry no sound params.
