# Sailing: sea charting

Milestone S5 (implemented in `SeaCharting.kt`, `ChartingData.kt`). Research: docs/plans/sailing.md section 5.1. `content/sailing` reads `Sailing_Charting_Core` (db 154)
joined with its type table, `Sailing_Sea` (db 201) and the tool recovery rows (db 161).

Sources: cache 2757 db 154-161, 201 (`dbrows 154`); OSRS Wiki https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Sea_charting (the `SeaChartRow`
templates, imported to `charting-tasks.tsv` by tools/wiki-import/sailing.py), /Sailing, /Captain%27s_log, /Spyglass.

## OPEN

- **OPEN-C1:** per-type behaviour data (spyglass cutscene camera, duck routes, drink effects, weather data tiles) is stripped from
  the cache and only partly on the Wiki; later S5 work needs the Wiki's tile-marker pages per type.
- **OPEN-C2:** Wiki `hazard` and `sea` names versus cache rows (join by name; verify there is no mismatch before S5).
- **OPEN-C3:** meaning of `Sailing_Sea.XP_LEVEL` (set on two rows) and the `HAS_CHARTING_RAPIDS` flag in the cache (unset on all rows read).
- **OPEN-C4 (new text):** the duck's route and speed are server data; the duck appears at the end tile after one tick per tile (`DUCK_TICKS_PER_TILE`) and is not drawn moving (NPC 15152 is unused).
- **OPEN-C5:** mermaid riddles (Wiki page "Mermaid guide", five guides) are not asked; "Dive" with the gear completes the task. Their text is on the Wiki and would be a dialogue import.
- **OPEN-C6:** the feedback strings of the generic tasks (`FEEDBACK_STRING` of db 155) are stripped from the cache; the messages are placeholders. Each charting NPC and loc position other than the task tile is also unknown.
- **OPEN-C7:** sealed-crate drinks (db 158 `DRINK_OBJ`, objs 31831-31905, NPCs 15160-15177 for the effects) and their effects are not modelled; drinking only completes the task.
- **OPEN-C8:** the task a troll hands out (31 troll places versus 20 known pins; the station is "specific to that location"); here the nearest unfinished weather task within 400 tiles of the troll. The Wiki's tile markers (`Module:Tile markers/Meaty Aura Logist.json`) are regions, not tied to tasks.
- **OPEN-C9:** the spyglass cutscene (camera, interface 936), hint markers (locs 59281, 59282) and hint arrows are not sent.
- **OPEN-C10:** whether sea bonuses are paid at once (here) or owed until claimed (varp `SAILING_CHARTING_SEA_XP_OWED_0` 5034); the sea completion varps 5028-5033 are not mirrored. The sum of the Wiki's sea bonus rows is 444,680, the page says 442,780 (1,900 apart); the rows are used as printed.
- **OPEN-C11:** Charles' tile (the hint tile of the spyglass task that looks at him) and his dialogue text; the rapids that appear when a sea is completed (locs 59376-59445, 70) and the Soup pet (1/30,000 per task) are not implemented; the cargo hold's tool compartment (cache db 161 only lists the tools) is the existing cargo code's.
- **OPEN-C12:** a loc or NPC on the root world cannot be reached from a deck by the click rules yet if the engine refuses cross-world approach; the handlers assume it works as the gangplanks do.
