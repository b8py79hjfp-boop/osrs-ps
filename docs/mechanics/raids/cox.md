## Layout: floors, rooms, pools

| Id | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CX8 | Three floors: two generated floors of rooms plus the **Great Olm** floor, which "is always static in every layout". Floor 1 is on plane 3 (the lobby plane) and floor 2 on plane 2 in the instance, each a grid of **4 columns x 2 rows = 8 room slots** (slots 0-7 and 8-15); a slot's column is `i mod 4` and its row is `(i mod 8) > 3`. Floor 2's start room sits in the same grid cell as floor 1's end room, one plane down. Music: Upper Depths on entry, Lower Depths on descending to the middle floor, Fire in the Deep on the lowest. | Main; RL (RaidsPlugin) |
| CX9 | **Floor contents (normal):** a floor has a start room and an exit room, **one resource room (farming)**, **one or two scavenger rooms** and **two or three combat/puzzle rooms** = 7 or 8 rooms. With two scavenger rooms one of them is always the large layout "with a skilling obstacle inside it". **One scavenger room is always guaranteed in the raid and always before the first puzzle room** (Main); dungeon layouts with no scavenger corridor before a room needing its drops were removed (31 Jan 2019). **Large** raids fill both floors with eight rooms: start, exit, one resource room, one scavenger room, four combat/puzzle rooms; duplicate puzzle rooms are allowed (two tightropes possible), boss rooms are never duplicated. **Full** raids use the CM layout (CX15). | Main; Strat; U (31 Jan 2019) |
| CX10 | Room kinds. **Combat:** Tekton, Muttadiles, Guardians, Vespula, Lizardman shamans, Vasa Nistirio, Vanguards, Skeletal mystics. **Puzzle:** Jewelled crabs, Tightrope (Agility), Ice demon (Woodcutting + Firemaking), Corrupted scavenger / thieving. A raid layout has **3 to 5** combat rooms. Room docs are not yet written. | Strat |
| CX11 | **Combat room rotation.** Combat rooms follow one of **four fixed 8-cycles**, and a combat room in grid slot `s` (0-15 across both floors) is `cycle[(k + s) mod 8]` for the raid's cycle and offset `k`: (1) Tekton, Vasa, Guardians, Mystics, Shamans, Muttadiles, Vanguards, Vespula; (2) Tekton, Muttadiles, Guardians, Vespula, Shamans, Vasa, Vanguards, Mystics; (3) Vespula, Vanguards, Muttadiles, Shamans, Mystics, Guardians, Vasa, Tekton; (4) Mystics, Vanguards, Vasa, Shamans, Vespula, Guardians, Muttadiles, Tekton (RL RotationSolver, which solves a raid from two known combat rooms). The wiki confirms "going either clockwise or anti-clockwise" and that puzzles are "on a similar rotation" (Ice demon more likely paired with Tightrope, Crabs with Thieving). 6 Apr 2017 added "more possible sequences for the challenge rooms". **OPEN-CX6:** the puzzle-room rotation (RL has none), how the cycle and offset are drawn, and whether the four cycles are equally likely. | Strat; U (6 Apr 2017); RL |
| CX12 | **Layout shapes.** RL's LayoutSolver holds **26** two-floor patterns (WooxSolo's table); each floor string is the 5 (or 6) rooms between start and end in path order, and the direction string has one more step than the symbols. Key: `S` scavengers, `F` farming (resource), `C` combat, `P` puzzle; `N/E/S/W` are grid moves. Checked here: every floor has exactly one `F` and one or two `S`; 24 patterns have 5 rooms per floor (16 with 4 C / 2 P / 2 S, 5 with 3 C / 2 P / 3 S, 3 with 4 C / 1 P / 3 S) and **2 have 6 rooms per floor** (`SCFCPC.CSPCSF`: 5 C / 2 P / 3 S, and `SFCCPC.PCSCPF`: 5 C / 3 P / 2 S). ## Scaling

| Id | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CX17 | The team's stats and party size **at the start** fix the strength of the enemies and the quality of the resources. "This scaling will not lower if players leave the raid." Monster stats follow the **player with the highest combat stats** (7 Mar 2019; before that the average). The party size sets the number of enemies in the **Mystics, Shamans and Tightrope** rooms, the number of scavengers and (smaller effects) crabs, herbs and the Ice demon braziers. In-room numbers belong to the room docs. | Strat; U (7 Mar 2019) |
| CX17a | **Scaling formula** (the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator's CoX module, the wiki's own implementation): n = party size (1-100), L = highest combat level clamped to 60-126, H = 55 + floor(44 x highest Hitpoints level / 99) clamped to 55-99. Offence (attack, strength, ranged, and magic unless the NPC uses magic as a defence) = floor(floor(base x H / 99) x (100 + 7 x isqrt(n - 1) + (n - 1)) / 100); defence (and defensive magic) = floor(floor(base x H / 99) x (100 + isqrt(n - 1) + floor(7 (n - 1) / 10)) / 100); HP = floor(base x L / 126), then + that x floor(n / 2). Challenge Mode then adds floor(50%) to offence, defence and HP, except Tekton's defence (+20% under 4 players, +35% otherwise) and the glowing crystal (no change). Clamps: HP 50-30,000, offence 50-5,000, defence 50-20,000; stats at 1 stay 1. Scavenger beasts and vespine soldiers use the "singles" rule: HP = max(5, floor(base x L' / 126)), stats = max(1, floor(base x H' / 99)) with L' = combat 60-126 and H' = Hitpoints level 55-99, both +50% in CM. Guardians' base HP = 151 + floor(sum of the party's Mining levels / n). Olm head 800 + 400 f, hands 600 + 300 f with f = min(n - 1, 50) - 3 floor(min(n, 50) / 8); the mage claw's magic is halved. Base stats are the wiki infobox values (maxed solo). Captain Rimor's level-scaling switch is not modelled there (OPEN-CX12 for it). `CoxScaling`. | weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc @ 701b2da `src/lib/scaling/ChambersOfXeric.ts`, `cdn/json/monsters.json` (GPL-3.0, facts only) |
| CX18 | **Scaled party size** (29 Nov 2023): the board lets the leader add invisible players that raise the difficulty "without requiring additional players"; Skeletal mystics scale correctly with it (12 Dec 2023). The scaled size feeds the same scaling as real players; whether it also feeds points and loot is not stated. **OPEN-CX11:** the maximum scaled size, the effect on the point multiplier (CX21) and on the herb and loot counts, and the size used for the "Team size" label and PB bucket. | U-CoX23; Main |
| CX19 | **Level scaling off:** when no party member is at combat level 115 or above, the raid applies a **level scaling** that talking to Captain Rimor ("Set-difficulty", earlier "Scaling") can disable (16 Jun 2021; renamed 29 Nov 2023). **OPEN-CX12:** the scaling formula itself, and what exactly "Set-difficulty" sets (the Rimor page lists the rename but no semantics). Cache: `RAIDS_CLIENT_HIGHESTCOMBAT` 12285 carries the highest combat level to the client. | U-KD; Rimor; cache |
| CX20 | **Puzzle requirements scale with the team's levels:** tightrope needs between 80% and 100% of the team's average Agility, drawn randomly; the shortcut skill level is the team's average level for that skill plus or minus 2, capped at 99; the cavern-grub count needed in the thieving room scales with the team's total Thieving level (CX26). Crab stun duration depends on party size: solo 50-60 ticks, 2-3 players 30-40, 4-5 players 20-30, 6+ players 10-20 (JagexAsh, 4 Mar 2020). Vanguard reset threshold: HP difference of 40% for teams of four or fewer, 33% for five or more (28 May 2020). | Strat; U-CoX20 |

## Points

| Id | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CX21 | A **personal** and a **team** point total. Client state: varp `RAIDS_PLAYERSCORE` 4609, varbit `RAIDS_CLIENT_PARTYSCORE` 5431 (RL reads both for its chat message "Total points ... Personal points ... (x%)" at completion), interface `RAIDS_OVERLAY` 513. Team points feed the unique rolls (lobby-rewards CR2), personal points the common quantities and the unique recipient. Only damage on NPCs spawned for the run counts (RC9). The points **multiplier rises with party size** "to compensate for increased difficulty". | Main; Strat; RL |
| CX22 | **Damage points.** Dealing damage gives points; "each point of HP damage dealt now gives an additional 2 points" (31 Jan 2019); points "scale more linearly with combat level" (16 Jun 2021). Exceptions: no points for damage to Olm's hands when it has regained control of them (the penultimate phase), and none for damage to a recovered abyssal portal; killing mini-bosses applies a **"decay" multiplier**. Thralls: U-KD's known-issues list says thralls gave no points and would "provide points based on the damage they deal" in the next update; no later source confirms it. **OPEN-CX13:** the base points per damage, the combat-level curve, the party-size multiplier, the per-NPC multipliers, the decay curve and thrall damage. Working assumption: **4 points per HP** for every NPC of the run (the 2019 "+2" read as 2 -> 4), no size multiplier or decay, behind `CoxPoints.DAMAGE_POINTS_PER_HP`. | Main; Strat; U (31 Jan 2019); U-KD |
| CX23 | **Cap:** personal points are **not capped during the raid** since 22 Jan 2025 ("Personal points are no longer capped at 131,071 during the raid"); the common-loot quantity still uses at most **131,071** points (= 2^17 - 1, which is the maximum denominator of the item formulas, lobby-rewards CR9). Additional points still count for the unique rolls. Each source below also has its own cap "which scales to the number of players in the raid". | Main; U-Bank25 |
| CX24 | **Storage unit:** building or upgrading gives **100 points per tier**. | Main |
| CX25 | **Shortcuts** (boulder = Strength, rocks = Mining with a pickaxe, rotten sapling = Woodcutting with an axe, in the large scavenger rooms): points = **5 x the level requirement** (example: 86 Mining -> 430). The 29 Nov 2023 post sped up the Mining and Woodcutting shortcuts so that they give "points comparable with the boulder shortcut". | Main; Strat; U-CoX23 |
| CX26 | **Thieving room:** **115 points per cavern grub** put in the trough, up to `maxGrubs = ceil(maxPoints / 115)` with `maxPoints = max(4500, floor(totalThievingLevel / 6) x 150)` (total over the team's Thieving levels); the minimum number of grubs needed to finish the room is `16 x players` (30 solo). Grubs stack to 28; a chest opening guarantees at least one grub; more grubs per chest at Thieving 50 / 75 / 100 (max +1 each, JagexAsh 12 Dec 2020). | Main; Strat; U-CoX23 |
| CX27 | **Food:** the player who cooked a bat or fish gets `4 + 8 x tier` points (tier 0-6) when anyone eats it and heals; +50% (x1.5) if somebody else eats it. Psykk bats from the thieving room count as cooked by the player who found them. Soft cap = **20 x players** points' worth, hard cap = **40 x players**, "with points gradually diminishing for each piece of food eaten past 20". The page states the formula for bats; applying it to fish is the working assumption. | Main |
| CX28 | **Potions:** potions the player made (or got from a boss) give points when sipped, more if another player drinks them; **overloads give the most**; the cap is **5 sips x players plus five more at a reduced amount**, counted in doses drunk, not points gained. "75 Herblore for overload, 90 Herblore for overload+." Sipping awards points correctly since 12 Dec 2023. | Main; U (12 Dec 2023) |
| CX29 | **Other sources** (amounts not stated): the **meat tree** in the Muttadile room, **Guardians** pushing a player back (non-linear cap in the party size, Mod Ash 3 Jun 2024, "the exact formula is not currently known"), **Jewelled crabs** (per successful crystal colour change, including crabs not used for the bounce), **Ice demon** kindling (cap about 6.5 sets of 28 kindling for about **6,050** points in a regular raid; a solo CM can reach 6,480 with the sequence 28, +28, +28, +21 then a second brazier with 28), **tightrope** (keystone used on the barrier). **OPEN-CX14** for every amount and cap curve not given above. | Main |
| CX30 | **Death penalty:** the dying player loses **40% of the points held before the death**; if the player had "none, or extremely few" personal points (**less than 5% of the team total before the death**), the team total drops by **5%** instead. 23 Mar 2017 raised the loss for low-point players. **OPEN-CX15:** rounding, whether the team total also loses the player's 40% in the normal case (working assumption: yes, the team total falls by the amount the player lost), and whether the player still loses the small personal amount in the 5% case (assumed: yes). | Main; Strat; U (23 Mar 2017) |
| CX31 | **Leavers:** a member who leaves early keeps the raid's scaling (CX17) and "the points multiplier and difficulty for that raid will remain the same" (Main's current text). **Conflict:** Strat and the 23 Mar 2017 changelog say the points multiplier "will decrease" with fewer people. Working assumption: the multiplier is fixed at the start (RC2), behind `cox.leaverReducesPointMultiplier` (default off). A leaver can still win a unique roll but the reward is **discarded** (lobby-rewards CR3). | Main; Strat; U-Run25 |
| CX32 | **Challenge Mode bonus:** completing CM inside the target time (CX46) adds **5,000 points per player to the team total** (not to personal points, 28 Feb 2019). **OPEN-CX16:** whether "per player" counts the party at the start, the scaled size, or those still inside, and the order of the bonus against the death penalty. | CM; U (28 Feb 2019) |
