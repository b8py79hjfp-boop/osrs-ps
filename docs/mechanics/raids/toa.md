## Conflicts between sources (raid-wide)

1. Lively Larvae eggs: "from 2 to 4" (Main, updated 30 Aug 2023) vs "from 2 to 3-5" (Strat): TI21, Main wins (newer descriptions).
2. Need Some Help?: "may or may not occur" (Main) vs "sometimes reduced" (Strat) vs a fixed 66% (cache text): TI9, TA53.
3. Chaos bundle adrenaline: 0-1 (Spirit) vs 0-2 (Strat): TA52, Spirit wins (dedicated page).
4. Penetration: includes "lower defence level" (Main) vs only damage and overheads (Strat): TI41, Main wins.
5. Logout: any logout is a wipe (Strat) vs the three-outcome rule with 25 Aug 2022 and 26 Mar 2025 changes (Main): TA47, Main wins.
6. Room points timing and caps: transfer at completion, 20,000 per room (post, Chest) vs live credit, Zebak 10,000, Wardens 60,000 (RL-TOA): TA35.
7. Warden phase-2 downs: stop after the third (post) vs after the fourth (RL-TOA): TA36, post wins.
8. Smelling salts run energy: once (Main) vs "repeatedly" (item page): TA59.
9. Path-level effect on defence: HP and damage only (U-Orn, DPS) vs "most enemy hitpoints and damage" (Main): consistent; TA28.
10. Stale client strings in the cache vs the wiki's invocation text: TA17.
11. Blessed crystal scarab: 8 prayer x 9 applications = 72 "over 40 ticks" (item page, Main) is inconsistent with a 4-tick interval (32-36 ticks): TA58.
12. Into the Tombs lists "Access to further runs of Tombs of Amascut" as a reward, while Main and U-Rel give only the Beneath Cursed Sands chain as the requirement: TA1, Main wins (the miniquest is not required).
13. U-Rev (2 Sep 2022) describes level 300 as roughly 2.5x more likely than level 150 for a unique, whereas the current formula gives 7,500 / 4,500 = 1.67x; the formula changed (28 Oct 2022 post, then 25 Jun 2025): lobby-rewards TR3.
14. Supplies infobox value 100 (wiki) vs cost 200 (cache); the cache wins.
15. Unique roll raid-level scaling: the 2022 post (400-550 at 1/3 effect, clamp 550) vs the Chest page formula (310 / 430 break points): lobby-rewards TR3 and TR4.
