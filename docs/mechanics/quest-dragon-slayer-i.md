## OPEN

- **OPEN-QUB10:** which variant of a Melzar's Maze monster group carries the key is not in the cache names (the Wiki says "the skeleton with the round shield" etc.); every variant drops it (`KEY_FROM_ANY_VARIANT`). The maze's other flavour (wardrobes, bookshelves with Melzar's notes, Melzar's overhead lines) is not implemented.
- **OPEN-QUB11:** the Trader Stan / Captain Barnaby charter refusals, Ned and Klarense after the crash ("Now I see why all the other captains said I'd be mad...", "Wow! You sure are lucky!...") and sailing again after leaving Crandor before Elvarg's death (the quest would drop back to the repair step) are not implemented. The Champions' Guild entry check and Jenkins' ship dialogue are skipped. DS9-DS11 close the Wormbrain, sawmill and shortcut gaps.
- **OPEN-QUB12:** the ship scene itself (boarding, the hull patch picture, Jenkins dying on deck) is not on the map; DS12 stages the voyage on the beach. The arrival tile and the volcano entrance are unchecked.
- **OPEN-QUC3:** messages the Wiki does not give (DS9 refusal without the Guildmaster's word, how long Wormbrain "has just been beaten up", the wall's refusal, the plank count after the sawmill checks).
- **OPEN-QUC5:** camera placement of the voyage (quests.md QU13).
- **OPEN-QUB1:** the maze doors, Thalzar's door are passed by moving the player through them. Ned's menu offers either the wig (Prince Ali Rescue) or the Crandor questions, not both at once.
