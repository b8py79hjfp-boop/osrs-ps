# Non-combat spells

Code: `content/magic/*` (`Casting`, `SpellRunes`, `UtilitySpell`, `Spellbooks`, `Alchemy`, `Superheat`, `EnchantSpells`,
`BonesToFruit`, `TelekineticGrab`, `ChargeOrb`, `Teleother`, `GodCharge`, `LunarCrafting`, `LunarBatches`, `LunarSupport`,
`SpellbookSwap`, `SpellbookAltars`, `Reanimation`, `ShadowVeil`; the Arceuus teleports are rows of `SpellTeleports`, the
Arceuus home teleport a row of `HomeTeleports`). One plugin-api addition: `ContentScope.onApObjT` (DECISIONS D-049).
Sources (OSRS Wiki raw wikitext fetched 2026-11-07 unless noted): the spell pages (`Low_Level_Alchemy`,
`High_Level_Alchemy`, `Superheat_Item`, `Lvl-1_Enchant` .. `Lvl-7_Enchant`, `Bones_to_Bananas`, `Bones_to_Peaches`,
`Telekinetic_Grab`, `Charge_<element>_Orb`, `Teleother_<town>`, `Charge`, `Humidify`, `Plank_Make`, `Spin_Flax`,

`Spellbook_Swap`, `Basic/Adept/Expert/Master_Reanimation`, `Reanimation_spells`, `Shadow_Veil`, the Arceuus teleport pages),
`Combination_rune` (CR), `Elemental_staff` (ES), `Altar_(Ancient_Pyramid)`, `Astral_Altar`, `Ancient_altar`, `Lunar_altar`,

no utility spell script. Messages without a Wiki quote are listed in OPEN-MU4.

| MU1 | A spell is cast from the spellbook tab. The click is ignored unless the spell's book is the active `spellbook` varbit (0 standard, 1 ancient, 2 lunar, 3 arceuus: the book components share interface 218). The Magic level counts boosted levels (`level`); too low answers "You need a Magic level of N to cast this spell." Spells that target a backpack item arrive as IF_BUTTONT: the engine only checks that both interfaces are open, so the handler re-checks that the target component is the backpack and that the slot holds the obj the client names (brief section 12). | teleports.md T1; RS Mod `Spellbook` |
| MU2 | Casting does not stall the player (no `delay`) except Spellbook Swap (SB3) and teleports (teleports.md T2). A cast that comes too early is queued once as a weak script and runs when it is due; a second early cast while one waits is dropped; walking or clicking removes the queued cast. Runes are taken and xp granted when the spell goes off, not when it was queued. Speeds: alchemy 3 (low) / 5 (high), Superheat 3, jewellery enchants 3, bones to fruit 1, Telekinetic Grab 3, orbs 3, teleother 10, lunar crafting as in the table, Shadow Veil 1; Charge, Cure, Energy Transfer, Heal Other and the reanimations have no Wiki speed (OPEN-MU2: 3 ticks, reanimations 4). | each spell page "speed"; Superheat_Item "Changes" 21 August 2014 ("queue up one spell"), Low_Level_Alchemy 2013 note |

## OPEN

- **OPEN-MU2:** speeds the Wiki leaves blank (Charge, Cure Me/Other/Group, Energy Transfer, Heal Other, the reanimations): 3 ticks, reanimations 4.
- **OPEN-MU3:** every cast animation and graphic is the cache name that matches the spell; the Wiki has no ids. Lvl-4..7 Enchant have no cache seq of their own, Charge and Spellbook Swap no graphic.
- **OPEN-MU4:** refusal texts the Wiki doesn't quote: alchemy on an item worth nothing, missing smelting materials, wrong enchant target, wrong obelisk, no unpowered orb, Telegrab with a full backpack, Teleother (target not accepting aid, cannot teleport, the prompt and its title), Charge too soon, Cure with nobody poisoned, "needs full special attack", Heal Other with a healthy target, Spellbook Swap title, Reanimation (not a head, wrong tier, not yours), Humidify and Bake/String with nothing to do, Plank Make on a non-log.
- **OPEN-MU5:** left out: lead / lovakite / cupronickel bars and the smithing catalyst (Superheat); sickles and quest jewellery (Enchant); unwatered saplings and Gricoller's can (Humidify); the sawmill voucher bonus (Plank Make); salve, m'speak and emblem stringing.
- **OPEN-MU6:** Telekinetic Grab's flight time ("Too late - it's gone!", level change fizzles, Tower of Life, farming patch) is not modelled, the item arrives at the cast; the range of Teleother, Cure Other, Energy Transfer and Heal Other is assumed to be the 10 tiles of other spells.
- **OPEN-MU7:** Respawn Teleport goes to Lumbridge until respawn points exist (teleports.md OPEN-TP4 style).
- **OPEN-MU8:** resolved: combat reads `SharedAttrs.MAGIC_CHARGED_UNTIL` (combat-magic.md CM7).
- **OPEN-MU9:** Superglass Make's bonus yield ("variable", 1.3 on average per pair, up to 1.6 with giant seaweed) has no published rule; 30% extra glass per pair is the stand-in (`LunarCrafting.SUPERGLASS_BONUS_PERCENT`).
- **OPEN-MU10:** Tan Leather's right-click toggle between soft and hard leather for cowhide; soft only.
- **OPEN-MU11:** the Dark Altar of Arceuus (loc 27979, "Venerate") and Tyss's dialogue to unlock and switch to the Arceuus book; the occult altar (loc 29150) and its four-book menu.
- **OPEN-MU12:** reanimation: the "dropped within 35 x 35 tiles" rule needs a record of where a head dropped (drops, trade, bank); the creature's lifetime, Slayer xp and level requirements, and whether the combat data makes the creature attack its caster.
- **OPEN-MU13:** resolved: pickpocketing reads `SharedAttrs.SHADOW_VEIL_UNTIL` (SV1).
- **OPEN-MU14:** Enchant Crossbow Bolt: the Wiki says the spell opens an interface to choose among up to 10 bolt spells; the cache's interface symbols have no such interface and the chatbox menu is capped at 6 options. Not implemented.
- **OPEN-MU15:** Stat Spy (interface 523 `dream_player_stats`, client scripts not traced).
- **OPEN-MU16:** whether a combination rune is consumed when only one of its two elements is needed (SP2).
- **OPEN-MU17:** Vengeance is handled by content/pvp (`PvpCombat`); Vengeance Other is not implemented (needs the same hook for another player).
- **OPEN-MU18:** Heal Group (level 95, divides up to 75% of the healer's hitpoints between up to five injured players, 90 second cooldown shared with Heal Other).
- **OPEN-MU19:** Resurrect Crops (level 78; success 50% at 78 Magic to 75% at 99; failure clears the patch) needs farming patch internals in content/skills.
- **OPEN-MU20:** Mark of Darkness, the demonbanes, Ward of Arceuus, the grasps, corruption spells, Vile Vigour, Dark Lure, Death Charge, Skeletal / Ghostly / Undead Grasp and the thrall spells: combat or timers outside this task. Degrime and the Sinister / Demonic Offerings (Arceuus utility spells) are also not done.
- **OPEN-MU21:** NPC Contact (interface 75 `lunar_contact_npc` and the contact dialogues), Magic Imbue, Fertile Soil, Dream, Hunter Kit, Monster Examine, Geomancy, Cure Plant, Boost Potion Share, the Tele Group spells, Boat spells, Ape Atoll Dungeon teleport and the minigame teleports: not implemented.
- **OPEN-MU23:** rune pouch, tomes (fire, water, earth), Explorer's rings and the Fountain of Rune as rune sources.
- **OPEN-MU24:** alchemy warnings and restricted zones (AL2, AL4).
- **OPEN-MU25:** autocast of the jewellery enchants (7 ticks), Plank Make (6 ticks) and orb spells (6 ticks): only manual casts exist.
- **OPEN-MU26:** Energy Transfer's refusal in single-way PvP areas and on recently engaged targets.
- **OPEN-MU27:** a combat spell cast during a Spellbook Swap doesn't end it (content/combat does not call `Spellbooks.onCast`); the 200-tick limit still does.
- **OPEN-TP5:** quest and unlock requirements (Lunar Diplomacy, Desert Treasure I, Dream Mentor, Tyss, Priest in Peril, Biohazard, The Great Brain Robbery, hard Fremennik Diary, Mage Training Arena) are not checked, as for the teleports.
