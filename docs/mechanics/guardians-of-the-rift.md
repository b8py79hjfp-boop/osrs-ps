# Guardians of the Rift

`GotrRewards.kt` (the Rewards Guardian, Apprentice Felix); hooks in `Runecraft.kt` (`craft`, `leave`). The quest tutorial runs the same
handlers: quest-temple-of-the-eye.md.

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| GR1 | **Place and entry.** The temple is a static area (tiles 3584..3647 x 9472..9535); the barrier loc stands at z 9483, everything from z 9484 is the floor. The tower portal (loc 43841, a multi-loc on varbit 13738) leads to the lobby (3613,9472) once Temple of the Eye is finished (varbit 13738 >= 130); during the quest (91-129) it leads to the tutorial floor, an exact copy shifted by (-1152,-3776) (cache `workbench` at 3611,9486 and 2459,5710). The entry portal in the lobby leads back to the tower. |
| GR2 | **Barrier** (locs 43700 / 43849 / 53263): "Pass" (op 1, also Quick-pass) crosses it in either direction; entering needs 27 Runecraft and, outside the waiting phase and the first phase, is refused ("closed while the rift is open", Wiki: red with an eye closed during a game). "Peek" tells the Great Guardian's power and the players inside; "Visual-effects" toggles varbit `gotr_colourful_fade_disabled`. Tamara's "close the barrier" dialogue sets a lock that closes it from the first phase on (the Wiki says it is unavailable on the themed worlds; ours is not one). |
| GR3 | **Game clock.** Waiting (`GotrConfig.LOBBY_TICKS`, OPEN-GR1) -> first phase of 200 ticks (Wiki: two minutes) -> the rift is open -> the rift closes or the Great Guardian falls -> a short respite (OPEN-GR3) -> waiting. There is no world tick hook in plugin-api: every player inside has a 1-tick timer and the first call of a tick advances the game (`advance`). Each game starts from an empty team: the players inside at the start count. |
| GR4 | **Power needed.** 250 stones per player in the game; beyond 20 players 200 each (Wiki). Every stone fed to the Great Guardian is +1 power. The Great Guardian starts the fight at `START_PERCENT` of that (OPEN-GR4). At 60% the temple rumbles and damages every barrier by 50 (Wiki). |
| GR5 | **Portal guardians.** Two portals are active at a time, one elemental (air, water, earth, fire) and one catalytic (the other eight), changing every `ROTATE_TICKS` (OPEN-GR2); the first two cycles never pick a weak-tier altar (Wiki). "Enter" (or an inactive one with its portal talisman) takes the player to the real altar (`TempleEntry`), the altar's exit portal back to where they stood. The altars of Lost City (cosmic), Troll Stronghold (law), Mourning's End II (death) and Sins of the Father (blood) need those quests. |
| GR6 | **Imbuing** (`GotrAltars.imbue`, from `Runecraft.craft`): at an altar entered from a portal guardian, guardian essence becomes runes (the usual multiples) and one guardian stone each (elemental or catalytic by altar) with the altar's usual Runecraft xp, at the same level as crafting; an uncharged cell becomes the altar's tier (weak: air, mind, body; medium: water, cosmic, chaos; strong: earth, nature, law; overcharged: fire, death, blood), replacing a lower charged one, for 10 xp (Wiki gives it for the weak cell only: OPEN-GR12). 1 in 250 per essence drops the altar's portal talisman once. Combination runes, polyelemental / polycatalytic stones from them and Magic Imbue are OPEN-GR13. |
| GR7 | **Mining** (locs 43715/43716 parts and 43717/43718 remains: 1-3 fragments, 43719 large: 2-4, 43720 huge: 4-6 guardian essence, 43721 fallen guardian: 2-4 essence, despawning after 28 ticks): 5 Mining xp a roll, the pickaxe's cycle is the roll time, no depletion; fragment xp stops after 250 fragments a game (Wiki). The Wiki's level scaling, mining enhancers and capes are OPEN-GR11. The east rubble (locs 43724 / 43726 at 3634/3636,9503, "Climb") needs 56 Agility and crosses to the large remains. Shortcut portals to the huge remains (west shelf) open every 200 ticks for 42 (Wiki "approximately every 2 minutes", "around 25 seconds") at one of four spots (OPEN-GR14). |
| GR8 | **Workbench** (loc 43754 "Work-at"): each guardian fragment becomes a guardian essence, 1 tick each, Crafting xp = level / 10 clamped to 1-5 (Wiki "Guardian essence"). Uncharged cells (up to 10, "Take-10"/"Take-1") and weak cells ("Take", one charged cell at a time; "Take-chisel") come from the tables. |
| GR9 | **Cells and barriers** (the seven cell tiles, locs 43739-43743, broken 43736): a charged cell on an empty tile builds a barrier of the cell's tier with 100 health (OPEN-GR6), 17/20/24/30 energy of both kinds; a better cell strengthens by one tier (7/13/22); an equal or lesser one recharges (2/5/9/15 energy, health 10/25/50/100); Runecraft xp 30/100/180/250 each time; recharging stops at 450 energy / 2,500 xp a game; a broken tile is repaired with 12 fragments for 25 energy of both kinds (Wiki tables). |
| GR10 | **Rift guardians** (essence piles 43722 elemental, 43723 catalytic, "Assemble"): a chisel and a charged cell make a guardian of the cell's tier, at most ten; energy 6/15/27/45 of that kind only, Runecraft xp as above and 80 Crafting xp. They stand in front of the Great Guardian; each wave they strike the oldest monster for 3 x tier (OPEN-GR8) and one of them falls from time to time, leaving a fallen guardian (essence) for 28 ticks. |
| GR11 | **Monsters** (leeches, abyssal guardians, walkers; npcs 11407 / 11405 / 11406 from the rift loc at 3612,9526): a wave every 8 ticks, more with time and after the rumble (OPEN-GR5); once they arrive (12 ticks) each hits the first standing barrier for 2 x (kind + 1), or drains the Great Guardian by (kind + 1) stones when none stands (OPEN-GR6, OPEN-GR7). Nothing attacks players; no monster combat is modelled (OPEN-GR9). |
| GR12 | **Great Guardian** (npc 11403, "Power-up"): feeds every stone in the backpack: +1 power each, 2 energy of its kind (3 for polyelemental / polycatalytic), 5 Runecraft xp, 1% run energy (the Wiki's special attack bonus is OPEN-GR15). Without stones it says "My power level is too low. Bring me guardian stones." or that it is fully powered. Stones cannot be carried into a portal guardian (transcript). At full power the rift closes and the players get their rewards after the respite. |
| GR13 | **End of a game.** Energy of a player is capped at 1,000 per kind and 1,200 in all. With at least 300 energy a won game gives Runecraft xp = base level x 45 and a point for every 100 energy of each kind, rounding up with the chance of the remainder (Wiki). Points are kept in the account values `gotr.points.elemental` / `.catalytic` (OPEN-GR10). Fragments, essence, stones, cells and portal talismans are removed when a game starts and when the player leaves. |
| GR14 | **Rewards Guardian** (loc 43695: "Search", "Check", "Big-search" = 5 at once, 1 per 3 ticks): one point of each kind per search, or 25 abyssal pearls used on it. Main table (denominator 140, or 125 when the player owns every essence pouch or the colossal pouch): the unowned pouches 15 (prepacked with 2/4/6/8 pure essence), air/water/earth/fire 4 each (400-500), mind 4 (250-400), body 4 (80-150), chaos 10 (61-150), cosmic 10 (20-30), nature 10 (28-150), law 10 (5-120), death 10 (5-120), blood 10 (5-120), talismans 16 (sub-table out of 35: law 1, death 1, elemental 1, air/water/earth/fire 3, mind/body/chaos/cosmic/nature 4; most noted), abyssal pearls 18 (14-16), intricate pouch 5, abyssal ashes 1, needle 1. Rare table rolled separately: Atlax's diary 1/20 (once), catalytic talisman 1/200, abyssal needle 1/300 (once; the needle used on the guardian replaces a lost one), lantern 1/700, a dye 1/400. Rune amounts fall with `(level / required)^4` below the required level (the Wiki's comment). The pet (1/4000) and the intricate pouch's contents are OPEN-GR16. |
| GR15 | **Felix** (npc 11404): the transcript's dialogue; "Let's see what you have" opens the Temple Supplies shop (shops plugin). Dye swapping and the lantern purchase by dialogue are OPEN-GR17. |
| GR16 | **Tutorial floor.** Same loc ids, other npc types (`tote_*`); no barrier, no timers, no monsters; the active portal is Mind until the first feeding, then Water; the second Water visit is by the portal talisman the first Water imbue always gives; the Great Guardian closes the rift at the third feeding. The minigame code writes the `gotr.t.*` account values the quest reads. |
| GR17 | **HUD.** Interface 746 (`gotr_hud`) is opened on joining and hidden on leaving; only its text components are filled (OPEN-GR18). |

## OPEN

- **OPEN-GR1** the wait between two games (`LOBBY_TICKS`, 100 ticks); **OPEN-GR2** how long a pair of portal guardians stays (`ROTATE_TICKS`, 150); **OPEN-GR3** the respite after a game (50).
- **OPEN-GR4** the Great Guardian's starting power (10%); **OPEN-GR5** the wave size, rate and cap; **OPEN-GR6** barrier health (100) and monster damage; **OPEN-GR7** the drain on the Great Guardian; **OPEN-GR8** rift guardian damage and losses; **OPEN-GR9** monster health and kinds. The Wiki gives none of these (it says only that monsters attack the Great Guardian, barriers and guardians, that leeches dominate early and guardians / walkers after the rumble). They are named constants in `GotrConfig`.
- **OPEN-GR10** the cache keeps points in varps `gotr_perm_0`, `gotr_rewards_perm` (layout unknown); we use account values, so the client's own point displays stay empty.
- **OPEN-GR11** doubling items (Varrock armour, celestial ring, mining capes), the amount's level scaling (about +25% at 99 Mining), crystal pickaxe charges.
- **OPEN-GR12** Runecraft xp for charging medium to overcharged cells. **OPEN-GR13** combination rune imbuing (polyelemental / polycatalytic stones, Magic Imbue, binding necklace) and the rest of the Wiki's "Combination rune tips".
- **OPEN-GR14** the four shortcut portal spots and the west shelf arrival; **OPEN-GR15** the special attack bonus to run energy; **OPEN-GR16** the pet, the intricate pouch's contents (tarnished locket, lost bag, lamp, blood essence), Atlax's diary and the lantern's effects; **OPEN-GR17** dye swap and lantern purchase dialogue, Apprentice Cordelia's pouch repairs (OPEN-RC14).
- **OPEN-GR18** the HUD's energy bar and portal sprites (their sizes come from client scripts we do not drive); the texts shown are ours. The atmosphere overlay (interface 745), the music (db rows `music_gotr_*`), the jingles and the "Toggle-talisman" op of the portal guardians.
- **OPEN-GR19** the barrier's three skins are swapped by phase; the barrier health bars and the barrier / guardian NPC sizes are the cache's.
- **OPEN-GR20** dropping a gameplay item: the Wiki says "Drop" destroys it ("The Guardian essence is destroyed as you drop it."); we do not change the drop op, items are only removed when a game starts or the player leaves.
