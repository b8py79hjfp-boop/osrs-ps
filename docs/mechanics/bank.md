# Bank

Scope: the bank interface and its item rules (`content/gameframe`: `Bank`, `BankLayout`, `BankDrag`). Behaviour source for
every row is RS Mod v2 `content/interfaces/bank` (https://github.com/rsmod/rsmod, ISC; clone read at 2026-11-06), cited as
`BankInvScript:line`, `BankOpenScript:line`, `BankSettingsScript:line` (files under
`content/interfaces/bank/src/main/kotlin/org/rsmod/content/interfaces/bank/scripts/`), unless the OSRS Wiki
(https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bank, `/w/Bank_placeholder`, `/w/Banker`) is named. Component, varbit, varp and param ids
are our gameval symbols (cache 2757) and `cache-dump`; RS Mod's rev differs from ours in component numbering, so roles were
matched by name and the op lists checked with `cache-dump interface 12`. Op numbers, sub-component ids and varps were then
checked against the disassembled rev-242 client scripts (`CS n` below = cache script n, e.g. CS 278 builds a bank slot's
ops); where they differ from RS Mod the client scripts win. No code was copied (DECISIONS D-029). A button
click is one IF_BUTTON packet handled in the player's input phase of the tick it arrives in; every row's item change is one
`PlayerRef.transaction` (brief §12), sent to the client as inventory updates at the end of that tick. A count dialog ("X")
answers on a later tick and re-checks the slot, the obj and that the bank is still open before it acts.

Interfaces: `bankmain` (12) as the main modal and `bankside` (15) in side slot 3 over the backpack (the borrowing described in
gameframe-buttons.md Q1; a once-per-tick `ModalWatch` puts the backpack back). Inventories: `bank` (95, stack always, 1410
slots in the cache), `inv` (93), `worn` (94).

## Not implemented

- **B4 (closed for wear / eat / drink):** content/combat handles `EquipRequest` / `ConsumeRequest` with the backpack ops' rules, without interrupting (the bank stays open). Rune pouch, looting bag and seed box ops remain OPEN-B4b.
- **OPEN-B4b** Op 9 of rune pouches, essence pouches, the coal bag and the gem bag ("Empty" / "Fill", varp `if1` = 261 bits 0..5, CS 2576),
  the "Empty containers" button (`bankmain:depositcontainers`) and the looting bag / seed box / herb sack: the bags exist
  (content/skills `Bags`, `RunecraftPouches`) but keep their contents in player-saved keys with no bank entry point, and a
  rune pouch, looting bag or seed box does not exist yet.
- **OPEN-B5b** The potion store (`bankmain:potionstore_*`, a second inventory of dose counts with its own scripts, CS 6080 and
  others) and the deposit locks (CS 7795).
- **OPEN-B5c** Bank tags need nothing from the server: the tabs live in client varcs (`banktags_t0_name` .. , CS 9580 ff.), imported and
  exported as strings by the client (CS 9607 / 9610).
- **B6 (closed):** content/combat refreshes the weapon category, attack style and special bar whenever the worn weapon differs from the one it last saw (on `AppearanceChanged`, which the bank publishes after worn deposits).
- **B9b (OPEN-B9 closed)** "Release all placeholders" and a filler's "Clear-All" ask first: `popupoverlay` (289) over
  `bankmain:popup`, client script 4212 `"title|text|cancel|confirm"`, the client answers with a count (nonzero = confirm, CS 4207 /
  4209); the bank stays open and is read again after the answer. Titles and texts are RS Mod's (`BankInvScript`).
- **OPEN-B10** Wording of the filler messages (B16). **OPEN-B11** Wording of the capacity tooltip (B19). **OPEN-B12** Defence line
  mapping on the worn page (B18).
- **OPEN-B13** (closed) Bank PIN: docs/mechanics/bank-pin.md. Bankers' "Talk-to" now runs the Wiki dialogue (OPEN-B3 closed).
- **OPEN-B14** "Configure-Charges" (op 9 of a bank slot, only with `bank_bankops_toggle_on`) and the bank item options.
