# Fishing

`content/skills/.../Fishing.kt`, data in `FishingData.kt` and `fishing-spots.tsv` (OSRS Wiki, DECISIONS D-036; the TSV is generated by
`tools/wiki-import/fishing_spots.py`). RS Mod has no fishing, so every number below comes from the Wiki pages named in the rule.
Shared rules (success roll, clocks, tools, messages): skilling.md.

## Rules

- **FS1: spots.** A fishing spot is an NPC; its type decides what its two ops give (op 1 = Net / Lure / Cage / Big Net, op 3 =
  Bait / Harpoon). The ids per type are the `id` lists of the spot pages: "Fishing spot (small net, bait)", "Rod Fishing spot (lure,
  bait)", "Fishing spot (cage, harpoon)", "Fishing spot (big net, harpoon)", "Fishing spot (Piscatoris Fishing Colony)" and
  "Fishing spot (Tutorial Island)" (https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fishing_spot). 123 ids are loaded: 108 from the Wiki lists
  and 15 more whose cache ops are unambiguous (source `cache-ops`); "Net" + "Harpoon" spots not on a Wiki list are skipped.
- **FS2: tick timeline.** Fishing is a 5-tick action: "a roll to catch a fish normally happens every five ticks" (Wiki "Tick
  manipulation" > 3-tick Fishing). Same shape as woodcutting (woodcutting.md WC1) with the 5-tick cycle:
  - Tick T (click, arrived, action clock expired): both clocks are armed to T+4. Nothing else happens.
  - T+1: the tool animation plays and the start message is sent.
  - T+4: the first roll; then one every 5 ticks (T+9, T+14, ...).
  - Pike (river bait spots) only: 1 in 5 cycles is one tick longer (Wiki "Raw pike", Mod Ash: "It's got a 1/5 roll in there").
- **FS3: the roll.** A catch rolls the fish the level allows from the best to the worst; the first `statRandom(FISHING, low, high)`
  success is caught, all failing = nothing (a "cascading chance", Wiki "Skilling success rate" > Cascading chances). Harpoon fish
  use the rates of the harpoon tier (FS6).
- **FS4: fish** (level, xp, low / high out of 256; Wiki "Raw <fish>" pages, "Skilling success chart"):

  | Lure (lure-bait spot) | fly fishing rod | feather | salmon 30, 70 xp, 16/96; trout 20, 50 xp, 32/192 |

  Bait and feathers: "For every fish you catch, you lose one piece of bait" (Wiki "Fishing" > Fishing rods and bait); a feather is
  used the same way. The item is removed in the same transaction that adds the fish. With both feather kinds the normal feather is
  used (OPEN-FS5).
- **FS5: big net.** Each cast makes nine independent rolls, in this order, each needing level 16 and a fish its own level:
  leather boots 10/10, seaweed 10/10, leather gloves 5/5, raw mackerel 5/65 (twice), oyster 3/7, casket 1/2, raw cod 4/55 (level
  23), raw bass 3/40 (level 46). So one cast can give several items; only the fish give xp (mackerel 20, cod 45, bass 100). Items
  that do not fit the backpack are dropped at the player's feet. Source: Wiki "Fishing spot (big net, harpoon)" ("A roll is made
  for each item on the table below, save for the raw mackerel, which is rolled twice", Mod Ash: "9 rolls: Boots Seaweed Gloves
  Mackerel Another mackerel Oyster Casket Cod Bass").
- **FS6: harpoon tiers.** Wiki "Raw tuna" / "Raw shark": rates by harpoon, listed as (low/high) harpoon, dragon, crystal:
  - swordfish 4/48, 4/57, 5/64; tuna 8/64, 9/76, 10/86; shark 3/40, 3/48, 4/54.
  - Dragon and infernal harpoons use the dragon rates (Wiki: "a 20% faster catch rate than the regular harpoon"), the crystal harpoon
    the crystal rates ("35%"). Levels: dragon 61 (Wiki "Dragon harpoon"), infernal 61 (assumed with the dragon, OPEN-FS4), crystal 71
    (Wiki "Crystal harpoon"). The regular and barb-tail harpoons use the base rates. The best usable harpoon is chosen as in SK4.
  - 1 in 5,000 shark, 1 in 2,500 swordfish and 1 in 1,000 bass catches are the oversized variant instead (big shark, big swordfish,
    big bass; same xp; message "You catch an enormous <fish>!"). Source: Wiki "Big shark" / "Big swordfish" / "Big bass" (Mod Ash).
- **FS7: requirements and messages.**
  - Missing tool: "You need a small fishing net to catch these fish." / "...a fishing rod to fish at this spot." / "...a fly
    fishing rod to fish at this spot." / "...a lobster pot to catch lobsters." / "...a harpoon to catch these fish." / "...a big
    fishing net to catch these fish."
  - No bait: "You don't have any fishing bait left." / "You don't have any feathers left."
  - Level below the method's lowest fish: "You need a Fishing level of N to fish at this spot."
  - Full backpack: "Your inventory is too full to hold any more fish."
  - Start: "You cast out your net..." / "...your line..." (net and rod methods), "You attempt to catch a lobster." (cage),
    "You start harpooning fish." (harpoon), filterable.
  - Catch: "You catch a raw <fish>." / "You catch some raw shrimps." / "...raw anchovies." (Wiki "Raw <fish>" change notes quote these
    for shrimps, anchovies, herring, sardine, trout, salmon, tuna, swordfish, mackerel, cod and bass; pike, lobster, shark,
    monkfish and rainbow fish follow the same pattern).

## OPEN

- **OPEN-FS1: spot movement.** The Wiki gives the rule: most spots move to another spot in the same area after between 250 and 530
  ticks (never: karambwan, dark crab, Civitas illa Fortis big net, boat spots; 100 to 300 ticks: sacred and infernal eel; 8 to 830:
  anglerfish; Wiki "Fishing spot", Mod Ash 19 Dec 2022). The data for it, the set of tiles each spot may move to, is not in any
  source we have, and there is no NPC relocation call in plugin-api. Spots therefore stay where the NPC spawn puts them.
- **OPEN-FS2:** the first-roll offset after a click (T+4) is assumed to match woodcutting; the Wiki only gives the 5-tick cycle.
  Barbarian-style "needs two consecutive ticks on the spot" behaviour is not modelled.
- **OPEN-FS3:** the fishing animation is replayed once per cycle (5 ticks); real animation timing is not sourced.
- **OPEN-FS4:** the infernal harpoon's use level (61) is assumed to equal the dragon harpoon's. The tool `levelrequire` param is absent
  for harpoons in the cache, so levels are constants. Infernal and crystal charges, the infernal "instantly cooked" effect and
  3rd age / trailblazer harpoons are not implemented.
- **OPEN-FS5:** which feather is used when the player has both is not sourced.
- **OPEN-FS6:** message texts other than the catch lines (FS7) are the game's wording as the Wiki leaves them out; no message for the
  big net's junk items or the oyster/casket.
- **OPEN-FS7:** not implemented: barbarian fishing (barbarian rod, heavy rod, leaping fish), the harpoon-less barehand method,
  karambwan, dark crab (needs dark fishing bait and the Wilderness), anglerfish (sandworms/diabolic worms), eels (slimy, cave,
  infernal, sacred), minnows, aerial fishing, drift nets, Fishing Trawler, Tempoross and sailing spots, shark lures, Rada's blessing,
  spirit flakes, skills necklace (casket odds), the big net's mackerel `bonus` rates, the Fishing Guild and other level boosts,
  clue bottles and the heron pet, and Tutorial Island's own raw shrimps item (the normal item is given).
