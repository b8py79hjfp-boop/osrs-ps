# Donator tiers and perks (D-093)

**Source:** https://wiki.elorin.org/wiki/Donator_benefits (fetched 2026-11-08), saved verbatim in
`util/src/main/resources/donator/perks.tsv`. Code reads the numbers from that file through `DonatorPerk`, so nothing
is restated by hand. These are custom-server rules, not OSRS ones.

| Id | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DN1 | **Tiers by total donated:** Regular $25, Super $50, Extreme $250, Legendary $1,000, Supreme $2,500, Uber $5,000. An owner records each donation with `::donated <player> <dollars> [note]`. The hub keeps an additive `donations` table, derives the tier from the sum, audits every entry and pushes the new tier to the player's world. One entry is capped at $100,000 (our limit, against typos). A donator tier is not a staff rank: it grants no commands over other players. | page; D-093 |
| DN2 | **Cosmetic perks** are on everywhere: the yell timer (min of the staff rank's cooldown and the tier's 30/15/5/5/0/0 s), the custom yell tag (Legendary and up, shown as `[tag]`; 1-12 letters, digits or spaces, our limit; changeable every 50/50/25 minutes), and extra bank space (+100..+1300 on 800). | page |
| DN3 | **OPEN-DN1:** bank space is capped by the cache bank inventory (1410 slots), so Supreme (+1000) and Uber (+1300) get 1410, not 1800 and 2100. Raising it needs a repacked bank inventory and a client check. | bank.md B1 |
| DN4 | **OPEN-DN2:** the donator chat crown isn't built yet: our cache packer can't pack sprites (modicons). | perks.tsv row 1 |
| DN5 | **Gameplay perks** only apply where `donator_boosts` is on: by default on dev worlds, off on live 1:1 worlds (`[commands] donator_boosts`, per world too). At value 0 every hooked site takes the unchanged OSRS path, with no extra RNG roll. Wired perks: global drop rate (every drop chance × (1 + boost), explicit empty slots unchanged); lamp xp; agility-ticket xp (flat); runecrafting runes (deterministic multiplier); mark of grace chance; burn saves; extra cooked food; extra fish; extra potion (unfinished potions too); hunter loot doubled; auto-noting of fish, logs and ores (not gems, essence or veins); bird nests into the inventory; bird house time and one extra nest roll; noted Blast Furnace bars; fewer raid points lost on death; Uber: poison, venom and dragonfire immunity, and 1 prayer point every 8 ticks (4.8 s, the nearest tick count to 5 s), both outside the Wilderness. The prayer regen starts at login, so a tier change applies at the next login. | page |
| DN6 | **Not built** (the systems don't exist here): zones, Town board, PK points, penguins, Pest Control, Slayer resets and cape uses, clue scrolls, crystal chest and keys, Nex, Godwars killcount, decanting, Bob, Tears of Guthix, Dungeoneering, Summoning, thralls, effigies, lizard shaman, Tokkul, stall automation, looting-bag auto-deposit, auto-bank, shooting stars, evil trees, Grifolaroo, Wildy Wyrm, Grenwalls, upgrade points, presets, daily rewards, restoration pools, Edgeville PvP, pets, Rune Sudoku, imbued ring of wealth auto-trigger, the extra log when firemaking (the row's meaning is unclear), the market tax cut and voting tokens. Burnt pages already reach the inventory (no change). | scout map 2026-11-08 |
