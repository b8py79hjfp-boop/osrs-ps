# Combat spell table (rev 242)

Research input for the combat implementation. Nothing here is implemented; no rule below is binding until the main
session promotes it into `docs/mechanics/`.

- Data: `docs/research/combat-spells.tsv` (62 rows, 17 columns, tab-separated), regenerated by
  `python3 -I tools/wiki-import/combat_spells.py <symbols dir> docs/research` (re-runnable, fails on any missing symbol).
- Fetched 2026-11-07 from the OSRS Wiki API (wikitext of each spell page, `Infobox Spell`), CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
- Symbol check: every `component`, rune, staff, `Seqs` and `SpotAnims` name in the TSV was looked up in
  `symbols/build/generated/symbols/tp/symbols/` (`Components.Magic_Spellbook`, `Objs` + `Objs$1`, `Seqs`, `SpotAnims`)
  and exists.

## Column notes

- `xp`: the Wiki's base Magic xp (granted on a hit). Offensive casting also grants 2 Magic xp and 1.33 Hitpoints xp per
  damage (Wiki "Autocast"); not in the table.
- `base_max`: the Wiki infobox "damage" figure, see the ladder below for the tiered spells. `0` means no damage roll
  (debuffs, Tele Block, corruption, Vengeance). Bonuses (magic damage %, elemental weakness, chaos gauntlets, salve,
  slayer helm) are not in `base_max`.
- `runes`: `Objs` symbol and count from the Wiki `RuneReq`. Elemental staves and tomes substituting runes are not
  modelled here.
- `staff_required`: objects that must be wielded to *cast*; empty = no staff needed. Autocast restrictions are in the
  autocast section, not in this column.
- `effect`: effects apply on a successful hit only unless stated. Drains do not apply when the target is already drained.
- `sound`: always blank. The infobox gives only an `.ogg` file name (for example `Ice Barrage.ogg`), never a sound id.
- `cast_anim`, `cast_spotanim`, `projectile`, `impact_spotanim`: **all symbol-matched** by gameval name; the Wiki gives
  no ids and nothing was checked against a client. Empty = the cache has no matching symbol or the spell has none
  (reasons in `notes`).
- `multi`: yes for the eight Burst/Barrage spells.

## Max hit rules

### Strike / Bolt / Blast / Wave / Surge ladder (Wiki, change of 29 May 2024, Project Rebalance)

The base max hit of any spell of a class is the value of the **highest spell of that class the player's Magic level has
unlocked**, not of the spell itself. The TSV `base_max` is the value when that spell is the highest unlocked one.

| Class | Wind / Water / Earth / Fire value | Unlock levels |
|---|---|---|
| Strike | 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 | 1 / 5 / 9 / 13 |
| Bolt | 9 / 10 / 11 / 12 | 17 / 23 / 29 / 35 |
| Blast | 13 / 14 / 15 / 16 | 41 / 47 / 53 / 59 |
| Wave | 17 / 18 / 19 / 20 | 62 / 65 / 70 / 75 |
| Surge | 21 / 22 / 23 / 24 | 81 / 85 / 90 / 95 |

Example: Wind Strike is 2 at Magic 1-4, 4 at 5-8, 6 at 9-12, 8 from 13. The Wiki states the table explicitly for
Wind Strike, Wind Bolt, Wind Blast, Wind Wave and Wind Surge (this is the same rule `combat-formulas.md` F12 abbreviates
as "Wind/Water/Earth scale with Magic level"). Other modifiers on the same pages: chaos gauntlets +3 on Bolt spells
(Family Crest), +max against elemental weakness. Whether "Magic level" in the ladder means the boosted or the real
level is unstated (**OPEN-S12**; the Magic Dart page does count boosted levels above 99).

## Effects

- **Stat drains (standard)**: Confuse Attack 5%, Weaken Strength 5%, Curse Defence 5%, Vulnerability Defence 10%
  (15% with tome of water), Enfeeble Strength 10%, Stun Attack 10%. "Can only be cast if the opponent's stats haven't
  already been lowered" (all six pages). Cast xp is granted on hit or splash for Confuse/Weaken/Curse.
- **God spells**: Saradomin drains 1 Prayer point; Guthix `1 + 5%` of Defence; Zamorak `1 + 5%` of Magic (Wiki, quoting
  Jagex Ash). Drain does not stack and is not reapplied until the stat is back at its base level.
- **Binds**: Bind 8, Snare 16, Entangle 24 ticks, +2 ticks per swampbark piece (max 14/22/30), "The holding effect is
  beginning to fade." near the end, Entangle 5 ticks immunity afterwards. Same numbers as `freeze.md` Z1/Z2/Z7. Damage
  rolls exist for Snare (2) and Entangle (5), Bind 0.
- **Ice**: Rush 8, Burst 16, Blitz 24, Barrage 32 ticks; ancient sceptre +10% rounded down (Rush unchanged, Burst 17,
  Blitz 26, Barrage 35).
- **Smoke**: 1/8 chance to poison on hit. Severity 10 (Rush, Burst) / 20 (Blitz, Barrage); an ancient sceptre adds 10%.
  Initial damage `floor((severity + 4) / 5)` every 30 ticks, severity falls by one per hit (see `poison.md`). The Wiki
  says smoke spells no longer poison poison-immune targets.
- **Shadow**: Attack drain 10% (Rush, Burst) / 15% (Blitz, Barrage). The shadow ancient sceptre additionally drains
  Strength and Defence (not modelled). Drain also lands on the up-to-9 secondary targets.
- **Blood**: heals the caster 25% of damage dealt (the full roll, even when the target has less HP left); bloodbark +2% per
  piece to 35%; ancient sceptre x1.1.
- **Burst/Barrage**: up to nine targets in a 3x3 around the primary target; accuracy rolled per target; barrage distance is
  measured to the defender's south-west tile (`combat-timeline.md`).
- **Tele Block**: players only (Wilderness / PvP world / Deadman). 5 minutes = 500 ticks, 250 if the target had Protect from
  Magic up when it landed (Wiki "Tele Block"). Target is immune while blocked. Protect from Magic does not change the splash
  chance. Removal: killing the caster, leaving the Wilderness, dying, logout.
- **Grasps**: bind chance on hit 10% / 25% / 50% for 2 / 3 / 4 ticks; chance doubled by Mark of Darkness (Undead Grasp
  100%); duration unchanged; a target under Ward of Arceuus is bound for only 1 tick (the Grasp spells page says 0.6 s, the
  Undead Grasp page says one tick). Immunity afterwards 2 ticks (`freeze.md` Z2). Swampbark does not lengthen it.
  Distance delay d = 1 (`combat-timeline.md`).
- **Corruption**: next successful hit has a 50% chance (100% with Mark of Darkness) to corrupt; prayer drain 1, 2, 3
  points on three activations 10 ticks apart (5 with Mark of Darkness), 6 points in total; cooldown 50 ticks; no
  corruption on an already corrupted target or one under Ward of Arceuus.
- **Vengeance**: reflect 75% of the damage of the next hit taken; once per 50 ticks; ends on logout or teleporting into a
  POH. **Vengeance Other** lets another player (Accept Aid on) do the same.

Autocast (Wiki "Autocast"; not in the TSV column): every staff, battlestaff, wand and crozier autocasts the 20 elemental
spells. Beyond that: Crumble Undead (skull sceptre (i), void knight mace, slayer's staff and (e), SOTD, toxic SOTD, staff of
light, staff of balance); Iban Blast (Iban's staff and upgraded); Magic Dart (slayer's staff and (e), SOTD, toxic SOTD, staff
of light, staff of balance); Saradomin Strike (staff of light only); Claws of Guthix (void knight mace, staff of balance);
Flames of Zamorak (Thammaron's (a), accursed (a), SOTD, toxic SOTD; not the Zamorak staff). Ancient: ancient staff, ancient
sceptres (all four variants), Thammaron's/accursed (a), Ahrim's staff (with set and amulet of the damned), Blue Moon spear,
master wand, dragon hunter wand, Kodai wand, Nightmare staves. Arceuus (Grasp and Demonbane): skull sceptre (i), slayer's
staff and (e), Ahrim's, Blue Moon spear, SOTD, toxic SOTD, purging staff, master wand, Kodai wand. Not autocastable: stat
debuffs, binds, Tele Block, Corruption, Vengeance. An autocast selection is dropped when a staff that cannot cast it is
equipped.

## OPEN

All of these need a main-session decision or a cache/client check before they are relied on.

- **OPEN-S1 cast anim variants**: strikes/bolts/blasts map to `HUMAN_CASTSTRIKE_STAFF` (1162), waves to
  `HUMAN_CASTWAVE_STAFF` (1167); unstaffed `HUMAN_CASTSTRIKE` (711) and `HUMAN_CASTWAVE` (727) exist. The same pairing holds
  for Confuse, Weaken, Curse, Enfeeble, Stun, the binds and Crumble Undead. Which variant is used for staffless casts, or
  whether the cache itself picks by weapon, is unverified. Surge has a single `HUMAN_CAST_SURGE` (7855).
- **OPEN-S2 Vulnerability cast anim**: no player-anim symbol exists (Seqs `VULNERABILITY_CASTING` 694 is the graphic's own
  sequence). The column is blank.
- **OPEN-S3 bind graphics**: Bind and Snare reuse `ENTANGLE_CASTING` / `ENTANGLE_TRAVEL`; only the impact has its own symbol
  (`BIND_IMPACT`, `SNARE_IMPACT`).
- **OPEN-S4 Magic Dart cast graphic**: `SLAYER_SALT_SPRINKLE_SPOTANIM` (327) is matched only by sitting directly before
  `SLAYER_MAGICDART_TRAVEL` (328) and `_IMPACT` (329). Slayer's staff (e) uses `SLAYER_MAGICDART_ENCHANTED_TRAVEL` /
  `_IMPACT` (1373/1374).
- **OPEN-S5 Mage Arena unlock**: the three god spells work only in the Mage Arena until cast 100 times (hit or miss), then
  everywhere. Where the counter is stored (a varp/varbit, not found) and whether the cast-count rule is already in the cache
  scripts is unresolved.
- **OPEN-S6 Thammaron's / accursed sceptre (a)**: the cache names `WILD_CAVE_ACCURSED_CHARGED` (27665) and
  `WILD_CAVE_ACCURSED_CHARGED_RECOL` (27679); which is the "(a)" item (and which is Thammaron's) was not checked, so they
  are not in `staff_required`.
- **OPEN-S7 Tele Block graphics**: only `TELE_BLOCK_TRAVEL_FORFAIL` (1300, the failed-cast travel) and `TELE_BLOCK_IMPACT`
  (345) are symbols. The cast graphic and the successful-cast projectile have no symbol. The cast anim has four seq variants

  `FX_SPELLIMPACT_CORRUPTION01`). `HUMAN_CAST_VICIOUS_STRIKE` (8974) is an unverified candidate, so the column is blank.

- **OPEN-S11 Vengeance**: `VENGEANCE_SPELL_ANIM_NOSTALLING` (8316) vs `_STALLING` (8317) is unresolved; the cast graphics

- **OPEN-S13 splash xp**: the Wiki states splash xp only for Bind (30), Snare (60), Entangle (70, hit 89); for every other

- **OPEN-S14 Wiki-listed rule questions not decided here**: whether Tele Block's 5-minute figure is exactly 500 ticks
